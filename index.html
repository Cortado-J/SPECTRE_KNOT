The Spectre Knot

A single‑strand Celtic knot on an aperiodic monotile

Draw the same ribbon on every tile of the spectre tiling shown here, which never repeats. Fit the tiles together and the pieces join up, again and again, into a single endless strand.

How we know →

The tile is the straight-edged form of the spectre, Tile(1,1), discovered in 2023 by David Smith, Joseph Samuel Myers, Craig S. Kaplan and Chaim Goodman‑Strauss. Read their paper: A chiral aperiodic monotile (Combinatorial Theory, 2024).

The knot, the ribbon drawn on it: Justin Roughley came up with the original idea of looking for a mono‑stranded aperiodic knot, and then used AI to search for it (2026). The proof, too, was developed with AI and has not been checked by a mathematician. How this was made.
