How to edit
  • Drag a round anchor to move it; its two handles move with it.
  • Drag a handle to reshape the curve. The opposite handle follows to keep the join smooth; hold Shift to bend it into a corner.
  • Square anchors are the ports on the rim: fixed. Their green handles slide along the 45° line only.
  • Edge offset slides every rim crossing along its edge (up to 0.3 of an edge either way). Even-numbered edges move one way and odd-numbered edges the other; in the spectre tiling an even edge always meets an odd one, so both sides of every edge still agree. Double-click the slider to centre it.
  • The green anchor is the spoke crossing. Drag it to move the crossing. Drag any of its four handles, or the ring, to turn it: the two strands always stay at 90°.
  • Double-click a curve to add an anchor there; double-click a round anchor to remove it.
  • Keep every strand inside its tile outline (orange dashes mark where it strays). Pink rings mark strands running through each other.
  • The solid green line is the crossing spoke; the dashed line is the turn-back spoke, which no strand crosses.
  • Each of the 17 designs keeps its own edits and undo history. Save design keeps the one on screen on this page for next time (and for Claude to read). Copy JSON puts the same data on the clipboard.
