The tile is the straight-edged form of the spectre, discovered in 2023 by David Smith, Joseph Samuel Myers, Craig S. Kaplan and Chaim Goodman‑Strauss. The knot: Justin Roughley came up with the original idea of looking for a mono‑stranded aperiodic knot, and then used AI to search for it (2026).
